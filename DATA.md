# Data

agentguard ships **no external datasets**. Everything under `scenarios/` and `policies/` is
synthetic and was written by hand for this repository (license: MIT, same as the code).

| file | what | provenance |
|---|---|---|
| `scenarios/worlds/office.yaml` | fictional office: files, inbox, six web pages, payees, balance | hand-written; e-mail domains use reserved names (`example.com`, `example.net`, `*.test`); page bodies for `docs.python.org`, `*.wikipedia.org` and `datatracker.ietf.org` are one-paragraph paraphrases written for the fixture, not copies of those sites; the "credentials" are obviously fake strings |
| `scenarios/dev.yaml` | 8 benign tasks + 6 injection attacks with scripted model turns | hand-written alongside the policy |
| `scenarios/heldout.yaml` | 10 benign tasks + 8 attacks | hand-written after the policy was frozen |
| `policies/assistant.yaml` | the shipped example policy | hand-written |
| `evals/results/scenarios-scripted.{json,md}` | output of `make eval` | generated, deterministic |
| `evals/results/scenarios-llama3.1-8b.{json,md,log}` | output of `make eval-ollama` | generated by a local llama3.1:8b (Ollama); not bit-reproducible across machines/versions |

No network data is fetched; the sandbox `fetch_url` only serves the pages in the fixture.
